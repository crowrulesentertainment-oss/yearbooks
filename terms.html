Terms of Service

Project scope, licensing, access, delivery and retention must be documented for each school engagement.

This is a placeholder and is not a binding or finalized agreement. Obtain legal review before accepting orders, payments, media or school contracts.

Project agreements should define

  • Authorized school representatives and decision-making authority.
  • Deliverables, revisions, timelines and acceptance criteria.
  • Ownership, licenses, publication permissions and credits.
  • Printing quantities, pricing, taxes, payment and cancellation terms.
  • Private access, hosting duration, renewal and account termination.
  • Retention, export, deletion, takedown and dispute procedures.
