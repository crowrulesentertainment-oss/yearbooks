Inclusive experiences

Accessibility

Yearbook experiences should be usable by students, families, educators and alumni with different access needs.

  • Semantic page structure and keyboard-accessible navigation.
  • Visible focus states and clear form labels.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Captions and transcripts for video and audio content.
  • Readable contrast and scalable typography.
  • Reduced-motion support.

This statement describes development goals. A formal accessibility audit is required before claiming conformance to a specific standard.
