Our Vision

Why A CrowRules Digital Yearbook?

The goal isn't simply to replace a traditional yearbook. The goal is to create a richer digital record of a school community.

Classic Yearbook

A Snapshot

Traditional yearbooks have an important place in school history. They preserve photographs, names, events and memories from a particular year.

  • Photographs
  • Student names
  • Class memories
  • School events
CrowRules Yearbook

A Living Digital Archive

CrowRules Yearbook expands the idea into a multimedia digital experience that can connect students, educators, families, alumni and school communities.

  • Photos and galleries
  • Video memories
  • Audio and stories
  • Interactive digital pages
  • Long-term online archives
