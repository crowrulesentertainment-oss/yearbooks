Stories worth remembering.

CrowRules Yearbooks is a video-first school memories concept built around cinematic storytelling, responsible production and digital preservation.

Our idea

A school year is more than a list of dates. It is the people, voices, performances, friendships, achievements and milestones that shape a community. We want to help schools preserve those stories through video.

Our focus

Elementary through higher education. Individual grade collections and whole-school productions. Public films when explicitly approved and private collections when access needs to be restricted.

Our promise

Your year.
Your story.
Your legacy.

Thoughtful storytelling, school-led approvals and a clear approach to privacy.
