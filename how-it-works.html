A clear, school-led process

From school year to story.

A guided production process designed around school schedules, approvals, consent and secure access.

01 · Connect

Plan with the school

Discuss goals, grades, schedule, locations, budget, deliverables and who has authority to approve the project.

02 · Prepare

Confirm permissions

Agree on consent collection, filming boundaries, student opt-outs, access type and retention rules before recording.

03 · Capture

Record the moments

Film approved activities, interviews and events according to the production plan.

04 · Create

Edit the yearbook

Assemble the video chapters, captions, titles and final edits for school review.

05 · Approve

School review

Authorized reviewers approve content, privacy settings, credits and publication destinations.

06 · Preserve

Deliver and archive

Publish approved public or private collections and document archive access and retention.
