Privacy & Student Protection

Privacy, consent and school authorization are foundational to every CrowRules Yearbooks project.

This page is a policy-development placeholder, not a finalized legal privacy notice. Do not launch collection of personal information or student media until a complete policy has been reviewed and published.

Our intended safeguards

  • Collect only information necessary for a defined project.
  • Obtain documented school authorization and appropriate parent/guardian consent where required.
  • Separate public content from private school and family collections.
  • Use access controls, private storage and short-lived signed media links.
  • Honor correction, removal, consent revocation and retention requirements.
  • Do not use student content for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Applicable privacy requirements, including FERPA, COPPA and Washington student privacy laws, must be assessed with the participating school and qualified legal counsel.
