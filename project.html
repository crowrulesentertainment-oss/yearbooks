The page plan is saved in this browser for this project. Database-backed page publishing will be added after the Yearbooks schema has dedicated page/content tables.

Interactive Story Room

Stories are saved in this browser for this project. No story content is sent to Supabase until dedicated Yearbooks story/content tables and publishing rules are established.

Interactive Photo Vault

Photos are stored locally in this browser using IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to Supabase or made public. Keep the original files backed up elsewhere until database-backed Yearbooks media storage is implemented.

Interactive Contributors Center

Contributor records are saved locally for this project. They are not sent invitations or written to Supabase yet.

Private Review Desk

The Review Desk is browser-local for now. It creates a private editorial queue without changing Supabase. Database-backed approvals, audit history and role-based review will be added with the Yearbooks editorial schema.
